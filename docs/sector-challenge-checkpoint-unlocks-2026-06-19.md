# Sector Challenge Checkpoint Unlocks

Sector Challenge start points are checkpoint starts, not individual sector unlocks.

- Start points are only eligible every 5 sectors: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, and so on while supported by the selector range.
- Sector 5 starts at Sector 5.
- Overrun boundary checkpoints start after the bonus sector: Checkpoint 10 begins at Sector 11, Checkpoint 20 begins at Sector 21, and Checkpoint 30 begins at Sector 31.
- Career checkpoint unlocks come from ranked Launch Run progress. Sector Challenge runs save separate local challenge records and do not update career best sector, XP, achievements, or main leaderboard progress.
- Because Checkpoint 10 begins at Sector 11, the career profile must clear through Sector 10 and record Sector 11 as reached before the Checkpoint 10 start point becomes available.

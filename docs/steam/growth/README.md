# Nova Swarm launch growth docs

This folder contains practical launch materials for Nova Swarm.

The goal is to make the game easier to understand, easier to cover, and easier to share before and after Steam release.

## Current assumption

- Steam wishlists: about 38
- Price: about $3.99
- Release target: around June 10, 2026
- Main obstacle: not enough qualified players have seen the Steam page yet
- Main fix: clearer positioning, stronger short clips, a simple press kit, and careful outreach to relevant people and communities

## Folder contents

- `positioning.md` contains hooks, descriptions, and Steam page copy angles.
- `creator-press-kit.md` contains public press kit copy and an assets checklist.
- `short-clip-plan.md` contains short video concepts for social posts and creator materials.
- `outreach-templates.md` contains polite email and DM templates.
- `outreach-tracker.csv` is a starting tracker for relevant creators, outlets, and communities.
- `weekly-growth-loop.md` is the weekly campaign routine.
- `steam-page-checklist.md` is the conversion checklist for the Steam page.

## Core pitch

Nova Swarm is a cheap, chaotic arcade score-chaser where every upgrade makes the screen more ridiculous, until survival becomes comedy.

## Operating rule

Keep everything honest, specific, and respectful. Do not mass-message unrelated people. Focus only on creators, outlets, and communities where Nova Swarm is genuinely relevant.

## Immediate next actions

1. Publish or link a press kit page.
2. Export a small set of short gameplay clips.
3. Review the Steam page against the checklist.
4. Share the game in relevant places with clear context.
5. Track what produces wishlists, replies, and traffic.

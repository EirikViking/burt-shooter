# Nova Swarm Patch Notes: Cleaner Combat, Controls and Hangar Clarity

Status: prepared for publication; not published

Scope boundary: changes after [Nova Swarm Patch Notes: Keyboard Remapping, Wonder Audio and Interface Fixes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4765070/view/685261553769906502) and up to BuildID 24632116.

## Steam title

`Nova Swarm Patch Notes: Cleaner Combat, Controls and Hangar Clarity`

## Steam body

```text
[b]Combat clarity[/b]

[list]
[*]Final deaths now show the impact and fatal cause clearly, clear lingering hazards, and transition into Game Over without the red vignette seam or secondary combat messages covering the moment.
[*]Game Over, results, and leaderboard transitions now stay composed while data loads instead of dropping to a black screen.
[*]Score, combo, survival, mission status, and high-score chase information have clearer priority during dense fights.
[*]Ace identity plates, combo feedback, first-run controls, and combat notifications now avoid the permanent HUD lanes.
[*]Ordinary full-health enemy bars are quieter while damaged, durable, elite, Ace, and boss health remains readable.
[*]Pilot Order progress respects its compact top-lane presentation and lower-priority messages are suppressed during life loss.
[/list]

[b]Controls and run flow[/b]

[list]
[*]Added Fire Input Hold/Toggle and Mouse Steering Off/On options, with local and Steam Cloud persistence.
[*]Fire input now remains consistent through pause and presentation transitions and resets safely after focus loss or a new run.
[*]Combo progress now carries through non-interactive wave and boss transitions and expires only during active play.
[*]Bomb targeting and opening boss inputs are more reliable around briefings and vulnerability windows.
[*]Overrun entry reinforcements no longer cause ship contact damage before their entry phase is active.
[/list]

[b]Menu, Hangar and accessibility[/b]

[list]
[*]Mode briefings now include an explicit [b]LAUNCH RUN[/b] action while keyboard and controller quick launch remain available.
[*]Hangar descriptions, traits, and weaknesses no longer truncate at common desktop resolutions.
[*]Viewing a hull no longer silently equips it. Previewed and active hulls are labeled clearly, launching commits the visible choice, and [b]VIEW RECOMMENDED[/b] is available.
[*]Ship Details keeps the Hangar context visible instead of opening over an empty black backdrop.
[*]Added persistent UI Volume, Flash Intensity, and Reduced Motion settings.
[/list]

Thanks for the reports, recordings, and difficult edge cases. They continue to make the swarm clearer without making it softer.
```

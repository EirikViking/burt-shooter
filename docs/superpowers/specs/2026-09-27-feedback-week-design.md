## Design decisions

1. Reliability comes before balance: reproduce and fix boss input continuity, whole-number upgrades, Sector metadata, snake achievement evidence, and duplicate achievement rendering. Earned achievements remain earned; do not infer a historic qualifying snake from end-of-run life count.
2. Preserve existing modes and account separation. Onslaught starts remain three first-level augments unless measured opening tests justify a future ruleset change. Improve preset usefulness, weak hull interactions, and Draft choices within the current explicit contract.
3. Meaningful choice: whole-number upgrades always supply their promised count within visible caps; no negative-only offers. Reduce repeated rejected offers without suppressing held choices or deterministic seeded behavior. Defensive sector effects must not unnecessarily disable primary weapons.
4. Combat rhythm: fewer repetitive snake waves, shorter low-interaction cleanup, useful direct-shot interactions, bounded chain propagation, more varied swarm/Veilborn encounters, and fair readable tractor cues. Avoid solving dullness by increasing health or undodgeable density. Test Pure and Tactical separately at sectors 51, 101, and 143.
5. Challenge: increase late-game boss pattern variety and purposeful paired movement without displacing active telegraphs. Keep the approachable opening and preserve current score values. Challenge targets are meaningful attack cycles and choices, not longer health bars.
6. Ship identity: improve a representative narrow, broad, and slow hull using existing weapon/focus/trait interfaces before applying any family-wide improvements. Reuse original assets. New behavior must have visible feedback, bounded damage, and a reason to choose it.
7. Presentation: steady colored friendly projectiles, restrained white cores and bloom, readable Rift Shard travel, repaired flame endpoints, quieter repeated hit audio, and no overlapping optional spoken cues. Maintain critical danger sounds. Preserve existing voice preferences.
8. Progress: group rank milestones separately while preserving unlock status, make counted starter hulls/loadouts visible, and clarify Serpent Duel qualification in every locale. Test real run paths including voluntary end, later deaths, Cloud restore, scene reopen, filtering and resizing.

## Title

Cabinet Wonders: Bigger, Longer, No Pause

## Body

Cabinet Wonders now have a larger framed presentation and linger longer during the natural break between waves—without pausing the game.

- Input, projectiles, timers, and wave progression keep moving normally while a Wonder is shown.
- Every Wonder keeps its unique authored artwork, now with more screen presence and more time to enjoy it.
- The previous paused presentation came from a good intention: rare Wonders deserved a clear, uninterrupted moment. We kept that goal through collision-free framing, careful layering, and dismissal before combat—not by stopping the action.
- If the artwork or a clean presentation window is unavailable, the Wonder is skipped instead of delaying gameplay.

## Primary research and design decisions

1. [Nex Machina's senior level designer on tension and pacing](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-maintaining-tension-in-i-nex-machina-i-): clear enemy roles and grouped patterns help players formulate a quick plan; compact challenges and brief recovery sustain momentum. Application here: audit actual opening footage for understandable priorities, gaps and competing presentations. Preserve recovery rather than saturating the screen.
2. [Returnal's audio team on creature identity and the mix](https://www.asoundeffect.com/returnal-game-audio/): creature mass/material and resonant character inform sound design, while the mix must communicate threats. Application: anticipation, recognizable individual voice, mass and departure; existing warnings always preempt the animal. Additional bus count is not a quality target.
3. [ZeroRanger's developers on iteration](https://www.lost-town.com/ZeroRangerInterview.php): early feedback and strategic purpose shaped their weapon and stage choices. Application: test the first and repeated encounter; a numerical variant or first-sighting reaction is not sufficient evidence of lasting interest.

These are design inferences applied to Nova Swarm, not proof that copying another game improves sales.

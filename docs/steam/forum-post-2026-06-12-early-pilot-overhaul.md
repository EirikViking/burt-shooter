# Steam Forum Draft: Early Pilot Overhaul Notes

Status: post-ready draft only. Do not publish publicly until Steamworks shows a safe preview and the publisher explicitly approves that preview.

## Subject

Early Pilot Overhaul feedback thread

## Post

Thanks to everyone who played early and sent notes. This build is focused on making Nova Swarm feel bigger, louder, and more "one more run" without turning the playfield into unreadable soup.

Main changes:

- Top 40 leaderboards are now supported throughout the game.
- Top 10 placements get stronger ceremony visuals and dedicated fanfare audio.
- Boss 1 is now Sonia.
- Boss 3 is now KurtBossEdgar.
- Bosses can call in 111 different support ships that help them survive.
- Support ships are bright and readable; killing them is a priority decision, not a cheap punishment.
- Boss deaths now use a new 100-line male agony voice pack plus unique visual/SFX celebrations.
- 1,423 additional unique enemy profiles are available from level 1.
- 177 extra mayhem enemy ships join after level 10.
- The 58 dangerous mid-ships still appear after sector 8 as the bridge between normal enemies and elites.
- Sector Start "One more run" now restarts from the same sector.
- The Windows exit confirmation dialog is removed.
- The fullscreen option is removed from Settings.
- The main menu now explains Launch Run vs Sector Start.
- The Hangar recommends the best unlocked ship before a run.
- Ten small easter eggs now appear through runs.
- 20 new hard ranks were added, for 40 total ranks.
- Pilot ranks now populate correctly in the Codex during live runs.
- The Codex has new coverage for ranks, boss support ships, and expanded enemy profiles.
- A new Early Pilot achievement is implemented in code with safe backfill for existing players.
- Level clear voice lines now use a 200-line random pool.
- Music was not replaced or altered.

Known setup note:

The Early Pilot achievement requires the Steamworks app-admin achievement entry before live Steam unlock validation can be considered complete. The game code and icon assets are ready.

Feedback I am especially watching for:

- Are boss support ships readable and fair?
- Does late-run mayhem look exciting without feeling unfair?
- Does the new Hangar recommendation help you remember unlocked ships?
- Does the How to Play screen feel premium and clean?
- Do any localized screens clip or show untranslated text?

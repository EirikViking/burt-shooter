## Steam/Forum Body

Hi pilots,

Nova Swarm has had a heavy feedback pass. The target was not just "more stuff." The target was better reasons to hit One More Run: cleaner information, sharper score chasing, louder boss moments, more enemy variety, and a Codex that feels less like a spreadsheet and more like something the arcade cabinet should probably not be allowed to know.

## What Changed

- The cockpit HUD has been tightened so score, level, rank, and chase targets are easier to read while the screen is busy.
- High-score targets are now more visible during runs, with sharper score-chase pressure.
- Leaderboards now present the global score deck more cleanly, including Top 40 support where Steam has enough entries.
- The confusing score-derived `LV` chips have been removed from global/friends leaderboard rows.
- Sector Start and run flow have been cleaned up, including same-sector restart behavior and longer sector-run support.
- The main menu and pause menu received major layout polish and stronger sci-fi presentation.
- The How To Play and Codex screens were improved for readability, pacing, and visual quality.
- Discovered Codex items now sort to the front, so pilots do not have to hunt through locked noise first.
- Bosses now have stronger names, lore hooks, death presentation, and support-ship pressure.
- Sonia, Sam the Misfit, KurtBossEdgar, and Misfit Galaxy are now part of the boss roster identity.
- Boss support ships and fuel ships give boss fights more tactical priority without hiding the important threat.
- Enemy variety has been expanded hard, including early-run variety and late-run mayhem, while keeping performance and pattern readability in mind.
- Powerup coverage and presentation have been expanded, with more ways for a run to swing into chaos.
- Audio moments have been widened across combat, powerups, level clears, game over, and boss deaths.
- The Windows exit dialog and fullscreen menu option are gone.
- Ship recommendation before a run helps pilots remember the Hangar is not just decorative furniture with engines.

## Codex And Progression

The Codex pass is not just counters and labels. More entries now lean into short sci-fi story flavor, enemy identity, boss mythology, ranks, support ships, and run receipts. The goal is simple: when you unlock something, it should feel like the cabinet whispered a bad idea into your helmet.

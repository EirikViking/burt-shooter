# Steam Announcement Draft: Early Pilot Overhaul

Status: draft only. Publish after Steamworks preview and publisher approval.

## Title

Nova Swarm Has Mutated: Early Pilot Update

## Body

Pilots,

Early feedback was blunt, useful, and slightly on fire. Nova Swarm had arcade bones. Now it has sharper teeth.

Launch Run is clearer as the ranked climb. Sector Start is checkpoint practice. The Hangar recommends your best unlocked ship before each run.

Bosses can call in 111 readable support ships, and boss deaths now use a dramatic male agony voice pool with bigger visual and SFX celebrations.

The swarm is wider too: 1,423 extra enemy profiles from level 1, 177 late-run mayhem ships after level 10, and 58 dangerous mid-ships after sector 8.

Leaderboards now support Top 40 everywhere. Top 10 placements get special ceremony. Pilot progression reaches 40 ranks, with new Codex coverage for ranks, support ships, enemies, Sonia, and KurtBossEdgar.

Also fixed: Sector Start "One more run" stays on the same sector, the Windows exit dialog is gone, duplicate combo text is gone, fullscreen was removed from Settings, and Pilot Ranks hydrate correctly.

Music was not replaced or altered. The game just screams better now.

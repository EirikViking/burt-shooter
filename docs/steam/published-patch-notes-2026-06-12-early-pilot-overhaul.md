Publish timestamp: 2026-06-12 19:42 CEST

## Headline Changes

- Boss 1 is now Sonia.
- Boss 3 is now KurtBossEdgar.
- Sector Start "One more run" now restarts from the same sector instead of dumping you back at Sector 1.
- The Windows exit dialog is gone.
- The duplicate combo readout is gone.
- Settings no longer exposes the fullscreen option.
- The main menu now explains what Launch Run and Sector Start actually mean.
- The Hangar recommends the best unlocked ship before a run.

## Leaderboards And Progression

- Leaderboards now support Top 40 everywhere.
- Top 10 placements get stronger ceremony, sharper visual treatment, and dedicated fanfare.
- Added 20 very hard new ranks, bringing the ladder to 40 total ranks.
- Pilot Ranks now populate correctly in the Codex during live runs.
- Early Pilot achievement logic is implemented with safe backfill for players who already played.

## Bosses

- Bosses can now call in 111 different support ships that help them survive.
- Support ships are bright, readable, and fair: priority targets, not cheap clutter.
- Boss deaths now use a 100-line male agony voice pack.
- Boss death celebration effects are louder and more distinct.
- Sonia and KurtBossEdgar have expanded Codex lore.

## Enemies And Mayhem

- Added 1,423 additional unique enemy profiles available from level 1.
- Added 177 extra late-run mayhem ships after level 10.
- The 58 dangerous mid-ship variants still appear after sector 8.
- Late game should now look much more unhinged without simply becoming unfair.

## Codex, UI, And Secrets

- Added Codex coverage for boss support ships, ranks, generated enemies, and expanded boss lore.
- Added 10 small easter eggs across runs.
- Reworked How to Play into a cleaner high-tech screen with better spacing and better punch.
- Added more menu animation/SFX polish.
- Level clears now draw from a 200-line randomized voice pool.
- Music was not replaced or altered.

# Nova Swarm Anti-Flop Store Plan

## Store Hook

Nova Swarm is a fast modern arcade score-chaser where you dodge readable swarms, blast a boss every level, and restart instantly for one more run.

## Three Differentiators

- Boss fights every level, with clear telegraphs and fast payoff.
- Readable enemy swarm formations built for dodging, scoring, and thumbnail-scale clarity.
- High-score chasing with quick play, instant restart, ship unlocks, and controller-friendly arcade flow.

## Screenshot Moments To Capture

- Clean first-level swarm with player bullets, score, lives, and pickups readable.
- Close dodge with enemy fire passing near the ship without hiding the hitbox.
- Boss fight with a visible telegraph and the boss health bar in frame.
- Powerup or wing-burst payoff with enemies exploding but hazards still readable.
- High-score or game-over surface showing restart motivation and leaderboard pressure.

## Ten-Second Trailer Opening

0-2s: Start with live gameplay, player moving and firing immediately.

2-5s: Show readable enemy formation, bullets, score gain, and explosions.

5-8s: Cut to a close dodge plus pickup or overdrive moment.

8-10s: Show a boss entering or firing a clear telegraphed pattern.

No logo-first opening, no lore card, no menu dwell, and no story intro in the opening ten seconds.

## Recommended Steam Tags

Arcade, Shoot 'Em Up, Score Attack, Retro, Space, 2D, Controller, Singleplayer, Action, Indie.

Avoid Bullet Hell for the first Steam page pass unless a later trailer/screenshot set clearly proves dense, fair, readable bullet patterns at thumbnail scale.

## Tags To Avoid

Roguelike, Roguelite, Procedural Generation, Story Rich, Narration, Twin Stick Shooter, Arena Shooter, Psychedelic, Third Person, Simulation.

## Features Safe To Claim

- Fast modern arcade score-chaser.
- Quick play from the start menu.
- One-input restart from game over.
- Boss fights every level.
- Readable swarm formations.
- High-score chasing and leaderboard flow.
- 25 playable ships unlocked through progress.
- 50 enemy-profile slots with distinct behavior signatures.
- Keyboard, pointer/mobile, and gamepad control paths covered by smoke or package tests.
- Optional story intro that does not block immediate play.
- Standalone Windows package evidence exists for the current release flow, pending final Steam-client validation.

## Features Not Safe To Claim

- Steam Deck Verified.
- Steam Cloud, achievements, trading cards, or workshop support.
- Online co-op, local co-op, or multiplayer.
- Roguelite progression.
- Procedural generation as a main selling point.
- Thousands of waves or content-count claims.
- Final Steam release approval before real Steamworks app/depot IDs, Steam-client validation, and human approval are recorded.
- Any guaranteed sales or revenue outcome.
